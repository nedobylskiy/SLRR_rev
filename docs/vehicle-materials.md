# Vehicle paint and reflections

The Java-installed part tree carries `Part.setTexture()` resource identities to each rendered part. The CFG `texture` directive identifies the replaceable paint texture; only the first matching link in that model's material texture table is replaced. Other links, UV coordinates, glass and interior atlases remain independent. Changes update the material map without reconstructing the car geometry.

SCX texture slots retain the base map, secondary authored mix and reflection map. The renderer alpha-blends the secondary map over the base and applies a separate additive legacy environment pass with the authored reflection strength. The MixTASpheremap pass follows `scene_raster3d.cpp` in the reverse; other layered material variants currently use this shared approximation. Reflection maps use their authored UV channel, or view-dependent spherical coordinates for channel `0xfffffffe`; missing generated-map links fall back to the Java-selected global environment. Painted body panels with an unbound reflection slot also use the global environment with generated coordinates, even if the reserved slot declares ordinary UVs. This avoids a zero-reflection hood next to reflective doors on stock Einvagen materials. Bound reflection textures retain their authored UVs. Painted panels blend a subdued environment contribution into the lit paint instead of adding a bright image over dark paint; other materials retain the additive pass. This is not full parity with every original material class.

This preserves the texture-layer structure needed for painted and decorated body panels. It does not implement the remaining native decal-painting/compositing operations.

The **Real reflections** checkbox enables an experimental local cubemap in both garage and driving graphics controls. It captures six 128-pixel faces around the active focus at most four times per second, hides the vehicles during capture to avoid feedback, and supplies the result to reflective vehicle materials. It replaces the legacy reflection pass while enabled, with environment intensity 0.5. One probe is shared by the vehicles; other cars and self-reflections are not captured. It costs additional GPU work and is disabled by default. Disabling it restores the previous environment and releases the cubemap.
